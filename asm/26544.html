67B0: Second dictionary (message words)
A second word list, in exactly the same format as 6040, holding words that only the game ever prints: the vocabulary of fights, weather and narration. The player cannot type these words (the parser does not look here), but messages refer to them the same way, by offset from 6000. For example BROADSIDE is $84F, DEFENSE $8A6, EFFORT $8CF, FATAL $90C, GLANCING $94C, INEFFECTIVE $9A0, BULBOUS $858 and PALE $A70.
Keeping these out of the main dictionary made the parser's search shorter and stopped players from typing words that had no meaning as commands.
