6DC3: Window separator pattern
Five pairs of bytes, one pair for each of five pixel lines. At start-up (6C27, from $6CC7) each pair is repeated 16 times across the screen at $5140, drawing the patterned bar that separates the story window from the input window below it.
6DC3 DEFB $C3,$C3,$2C,$34,$10,$08,$2C,$34,$C3,$C3
