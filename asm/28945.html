7111: Report the action (if not inside a quotation)
Marks the command as really happening (B5EC=0, $B5EB=1) and, unless the parser is in the middle of a quotation ($B60B), prints the report of the action with 7122. Returns with A=0.
7111 XOR A The action has not yet been shown to work...
7112 LD ($B5EC),A
7115 INC A ...but it really is being carried out now, not just tried
7116 LD ($B5EB),A
7119 LD A,($B60B) Are we in the middle of a quotation (an order being given to a character)?
711C AND A
711D CALL Z,$7122 If not, report the action in words ("you attack thorin.")
7120 XOR A Clear A before returning
7121 RET
