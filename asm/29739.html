742B: Print an object's name from its word list
Prints the name of the thing whose word list is at HL (adjectives followed by the noun) by calling 9E1F with IY pointing at the list.
Input
HL Word list
742B PUSH IY Save the caller's IY
742D PUSH HL IY = the word-list address given in HL
742E POP IY
7430 CALL $9E1F Print the noun with its adjectives
7433 POP IY Restore the caller's IY
7435 RET
