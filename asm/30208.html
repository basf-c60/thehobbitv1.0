7600: Scroll the input window up
Scrolls the lower window (the four character rows starting at $5060, in the bottom third of the screen) up by one row, a pixel line at a time with LDIR, and then clears the bottom row at $50E0 by printing 32 spaces with 766D. All registers are preserved.
