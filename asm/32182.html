7DB6: Parser: a direction
A direction on its own ('north') is a complete movement command: if a verb may start here (bit 1 of E), it is treated as GO NORTH (7DFB). After a verb, a direction is stored as the frame's particle, like an adverb (7DBC): bit 2 of E must allow it, and it is put at offset 2 of the frame (7FE2). The entry at 7DBC is the handler for adverbs.
7DB6 BIT 1,E May a verb start here?
7DB8 JR NZ,$7DFB Yes: treat the direction as "go <direction>"
7DBA LD D,$00 Otherwise it is used like an adverb
7DBC BIT 2,E ADVERB: is an adverb allowed here?
7DBE JP Z,$7FF3 No: syntax error
7DC1 LD A,($B609) Under ALL EXCEPT...
7DC4 CP $02
7DC6 JR NZ,$7DCB
7DC8 CALL $7F22 ...start a new frame for it
7DCB RES 2,E No more adverbs
7DCD LD L,$02 Store the word at offset 2 of the frame
7DCF CALL $7FE2
7DD2 JP $7C7E Next noun phrase
