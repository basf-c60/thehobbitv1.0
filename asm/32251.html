7DFB: Parser: a verb
The entry at $7DFB (from 7DB6) supplies the verb GO ($30 class) for a bare direction; the entry at $7DFD handles verbs typed by the player.
If a verb arrives when a command has already been started and there has been no AND, the parser backtracks: the state saved by the last AND (7DD5) is restored and the AND is reinterpreted as THEN, splitting the line into two commands.
Otherwise the verb must be allowed here (bit 1 of E). It is stored at offset 0 of the frame (7FDE). GO and RUN look ahead (from $7E31) past any adverbs: if the next word is a direction, the verb is dropped and the direction stored as the particle, so that 'go north' and 'north' produce the same frame.
