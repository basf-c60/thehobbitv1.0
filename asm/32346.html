7E5A: Parser: an article
Articles (A, AN, THE and the like) are ignored, apart from clearing bit 4 of E; the parser goes straight on to the next word (7C88).
7E5A RES 4,E No article is expected next
7E5C JP $7C88 Go back for the next word: the article itself is simply ignored
