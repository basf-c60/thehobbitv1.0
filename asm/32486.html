7EE6: Parser: store a preposition
Counts the prepositions in the current noun phrase ($7C44) and stores the token BC in the first empty preposition slot ($7C45 or $7C47). A third preposition is a syntax error (7FEE).
7EE6 LD HL,$7C44 HL = the count of prepositions seen in this noun phrase so far
7EE9 INC (HL) One more
7EEA LD A,(HL)
7EEB CP $03 Is that three or more?
7EED JP NC,$7FEE A third preposition makes no sense: syntax error
7EF0 LD HL,$7C45 Otherwise HL = the first or second preposition slot...
