7FC5: Copy part of one frame to another
Copies C bytes at offset DE from the frame at IX to the same offset in the frame at IY. Entry points: $7FC5 copies 6 bytes (a noun and two adjectives), $7FC9 copies 10 (a whole noun phrase) and $7FCD copies 2 (one word). Used by 7F81 and 7CC0 to share words between commands.
