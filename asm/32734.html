7FDE: Parser: store the verb
Clears bit 1 of E (no more verbs allowed) and stores the token BC at offset 0 of the current frame. The entry at 7FE2 stores BC at offset L instead (used for the particle at offset 2).
Input
BC Token
Output
E Updated state
7FDE RES 1,E No more verbs allowed on this line, until the next command starts
7FE0 LD L,$00 L = the offset within the frame to store at (0 for the verb, or wherever the caller set it for the particle)
This entry point is used by the routines at 7DB6 and 7DFB.
