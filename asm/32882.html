8072: EXCEPT
EXCEPT (or BUT) is only allowed straight after ALL ($B609=1). It sets $B609 to 2 and bit 7 of the current frame's verb, so that the command applies to everything except the objects that follow.
8072 LD A,($B609) Is ALL actually in force?
8075 CP $01
8077 JP NZ,$7FF3 No: EXCEPT makes no sense here - syntax error
807A LD A,$02 Yes: switch to ALL EXCEPT
807C LD ($B609),A
807F LD A,(IY+$01) Mark the CURRENT frame's verb with the EXCEPT bit...
8082 OR $80
8084 LD (IY+$01),A ...so 7E6C knows any further noun phrases are exceptions, not extra targets
8087 JP $7C7E Go back to reading the next word
