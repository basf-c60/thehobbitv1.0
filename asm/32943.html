80AF: Store the noun phrase in the frame (jump)
Jumps to $7F02 (second noun phrase) if Z is set, otherwise to $7F1A (first noun phrase); see 7EF5.
80AF JP Z,$7F02 First phrase already taken: store in the second
80B2 JP $7F1A Otherwise store in the first
