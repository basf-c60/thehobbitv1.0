8158: HELP
HELP gives a hint that depends on where Bilbo is. His room is looked up in the table at 8185; if it is there, that message is printed, otherwise the general one at B358: 'you're doing fine.' The message goes to the lower window. HELP is ignored if a character is the actor (for example inside SAY TO).
