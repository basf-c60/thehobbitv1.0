8569: Decode the flags of the action
Turns the flag nibbles of the action table entry (see 70EA) into separate variables: $B601 (bit 6 of B60E: the action is allowed in the dark), $B5FF (bit 0 of $B60D: the objects need not be within reach), $B5EF (bit 7: the target is a room rather than an object) and $B5F0 (bit 6: the instrument is a room).
8569 LD A,($B60E) A = the action's second set of flags
856C AND $40 Is it allowed in the dark?
856E LD ($B601),A
8571 LD A,($B60D) A = the action's main flags
8574 LD B,A
8575 AND $01 Do the objects need not be within reach?
8577 LD ($B5FF),A
857A LD A,B
857B AND $80 Is the target actually a room, rather than an object?
857D JR Z,$8581
857F LD A,$01
8581 LD ($B5EF),A
8584 LD A,B Is the instrument actually a room too?
8585 AND $40
8587 JR Z,$858B
8589 LD A,$01
858B LD ($B5F0),A
858E RET
