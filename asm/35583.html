8AFF: Move an attribute address right
Increases HL, unless that would move it out of the attribute file.
8AFF PUSH AF Save AF
8B00 INC HL Move HL one cell right
8B01 LD A,H Did that go past the right edge?
8B02 CP $5A
8B04 JR NZ,$8B07
8B06 DEC HL If so, undo the move: stay put
8B07 POP AF Restore AF
8B08 RET
