8B18: Move the drawing position down
Decreases E, unless it would go below 0. Returns with Z set at the edge.
8B18 DEC E y one lower
8B19 BIT 7,E Did that go below 0, off the bottom of the picture area?
8B1B JR Z,$8B13 No: continue at the shared ending (reused from 8B09)
8B1D INC E Yes: undo the move
8B1E RET Z stays set, telling the caller the edge was reached
