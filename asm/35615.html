8B1F: Move the drawing position right
Increases D (x), unless it would go past 255. Returns with Z set at the edge.
8B1F INC D x one higher
8B20 RET NZ Still within 0-255: done
8B21 DEC D Wrapped past 255: undo the move
8B22 LD H,A Save A for a moment...
8B23 XOR A ...set Z, to tell the caller the edge was reached...
8B24 LD A,H ...then restore A
8B25 RET
