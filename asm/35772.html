8BBC: Get the screen address of a pixel
Converts the picture coordinates (D,E) to a screen address HL and a bit mask A. The y coordinate is turned upside down (127-E), because pictures are drawn with y measured upwards, and then split up in the usual way for the Spectrum's interleaved screen layout.
Input
D x
E y
Output
HL Screen address
A Bit mask
8BBC LD A,$7F Turn y upside down: row = 127 - y
