8E5A: LOOK ACROSS (a river)
Finds the exit that passes through the target (9E6E). If it leads anywhere, and the room on the other side is lit, the actor is moved there for a moment, the room is described ('you see ...', 9589), and the actor is put back. If the far side is dark: 'it is dark.' This is how you can see what is on the other bank of the river, or behind a door, without going there.
