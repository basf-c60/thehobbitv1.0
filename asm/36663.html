8F37: ENTER and GO INTO
Finds the exit of the current room that leads to the room named as the target (9E76) and goes through it (8EA0), so 'go into the tunnel' works without naming a direction.
8F37 LD A,($B5D9) A = the target (the room named, e.g. "the tunnel")
8F3A CALL $9E76 Find the exit that leads there
8F3D JP $8EA0 Go through it
