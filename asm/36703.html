8F5F: THROW AT
The default handler for THROW X AT Y. After checking that the actor can lift the thing thrown (8C86), it becomes an attack: if the target is alive, the fight routine (90DB, ATTACK) is used, otherwise the breaking routine (9257, STRIKE), in both cases with the thrown object as the weapon (9E93). Afterwards the thrown object lands at the target's feet (its holder is cleared) and the target reacts as if attacked (953F).
