90B4: Check that a target may be attacked (sides)
Characters on the same side do not fight each other. The target's side bits (4-6 of byte 4 of its record) are compared with the attacker's; if they have any in common, the attack is quietly ruled out: the 'would work' flag is cleared and the attack routine's caller is abandoned.
But the player is allowed to attack a friend. When Bilbo is the attacker and the target is on his side (bit 4), that bit is cleared in the target's record first - permanently. So hitting Thorin or Gandalf takes them off Bilbo's side for the rest of the game, which is what lets them fight back (and, since no one is on the same side any more, lets other characters attack them too).
90B4 LD IX,($B5F8) IX = the target's record
90B8 LD A,($B5DB) Is the attacker Bilbo?
90BB AND A
90BC JR NZ,$90C8 No: just compare sides
90BE BIT 4,(IX+$04) Is the target on Bilbo's side (bit 4)?
90C2 JR Z,$90C8 No
90C4 RES 4,(IX+$04) Yes: it is not any more - for good
90C8 LD A,(IX+$04) A = the target's side bits...
90CB AND $70
90CD LD IX,($B5FC) IX = the attacker's record
90D1 AND (IX+$04) ...in common with the attacker's?
90D4 RET Z None: the attack may go ahead
90D5 POP HL Same side: drop the caller's return address...
90D6 XOR A ...and record that the attack would not work
90D7 LD ($B5EC),A
90DA RET
