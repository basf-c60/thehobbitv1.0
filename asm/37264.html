9190: Wound messages
Addresses of the messages printed when a blow wounds but does not kill, indexed by twice the margin by which the attack beat the defence (see 90DB). Entry 0 is never used (a margin of 0 means the blow was wasted). The small margins give the mildest messages and the larger margins the most violent ones, such as 'you hit thorin hard on the shoulder - thorin staggers and almost falls'.
The table has 16 entries (9190-$91AF), but the margin can be 16, which reads past the end into the code at 91B0 (see the bug note in 90DB).
