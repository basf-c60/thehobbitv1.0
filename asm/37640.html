9308: GIVE TO
The actor must be carrying the target (9BCD; otherwise 'you are not carrying it'). The receiver must be able to carry it: the target's weight plus what the receiver already carries (9C42) must not exceed the receiver's capacity, or 'X is carrying too much'. Then the receiver becomes the target's holder, the target moves to the receiver's room, and 9B38 tells anyone who sees it.
