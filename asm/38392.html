95F8: Wait for a key after a picture
Waits until a key is pressed, then resets the border to white (the picture may have changed it).
95F8 XOR A Is a key already being held down (so we should wait for it to be released first)?
95F9 IN A,($FE)
95FB AND $1F
95FD CP $1F Yes: keep waiting
95FF JR Z,$95F8
9601 LD A,$07 A key has been pressed: reset the border to white (a picture may have changed its colour)
9603 OUT ($FE),A
9605 RET
