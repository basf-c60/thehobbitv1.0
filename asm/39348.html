99B4: Choose a behaviour at random
Picks a random entry (9BF4) from the character's reaction table (the address at offset 4 of its table entry) and makes it the character's current behaviour program. The range is the smaller of byte 1 of the instruction and byte 1 of the character's table entry. The entry at $99C3 selects entry E instead.
