9C3D: Total size of an object's contents
As 9C42, but adding up the objects' sizes (byte 2) instead of their weights.
9C3D PUSH BC TOTAL SIZE: save the caller's BC
9C3E LD B,$01 B=1 tells the shared routine below to add up sizes rather than weights
9C40 JR $9C45 Join the weight routine, which does the actual work
