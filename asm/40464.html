9E10: Print an object's name
Prints the words of the object whose record is at IY (9E1F on its word slots).
9E10 PUSH IY Save the caller's IY and DE
9E12 PUSH DE
9E13 LD DE,$0008 An object's own word slots start 8 bytes into its record
9E16 ADD IY,DE
9E18 CALL $9E1F Print the noun and its adjectives
9E1B POP DE Restore the caller's DE and IY
9E1C POP IY
9E1E RET
