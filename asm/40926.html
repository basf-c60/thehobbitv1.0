9FDE: Get the start of a room's exits
Returns IX pointing seven bytes into room A's record and BC=3, ready for stepping through its exits.
9FDE CALL $9B0C IX = room A's own record
9FE1 LD BC,$0007 A room's exits start 7 bytes into its record
9FE4 ADD IX,BC
9FE6 LD BC,$0003 BC = 3: the size of one exit, ready for the caller to step through them
9FE9 RET
