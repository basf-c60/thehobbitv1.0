A0F8: Is the object alive?
Returns with Z set if the object at IX is a living thing (bit 6 of its flags set) and not dead (bit 3 clear). Used by SHOOT (8FE0).
A0F8 LD A,(IX+$07) A = the object's own flags
A0FB AND $48 Keep just "alive" and "dead"
A0FD CP $40 Alive and not dead?
A0FF RET Z reflects the answer
