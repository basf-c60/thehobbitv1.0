A1FD: Handler for the fast river: SWIM
Swimming across the fast river: the exit through the river is found (9E6E); if it leads anywhere, the river is opened for a moment and the actor goes through it with an ordinary move (8D19, at 8D27), then the river is closed again. The entry at $A20C is the handler used by many doors and containers for their OPEN action when they are chained to a movement.
A1FD CALL $9E6E Find the exit that passes through the river
A200 LD A,(IX+$02) Does it lead anywhere?
A203 CP $00
A205 JR NZ,$A20B Yes: swim across, below
A207 CALL $9C99 No: dry run? Then stop here: the attempt itself is possible
A20A RET (falls through to failing normally otherwise)
A20B LD A,(IX+$00) A = the exit's own direction
A20E LD HL,($B5D8) Save the current action for a moment...
A211 PUSH HL
A212 LD ($B5D8),A ...and use the direction as the movement action instead
A215 LD IX,($B5F8) IX = the river's own record
A219 SET 5,(IX+$07) Open it, just long enough to move through it
A21D PUSH IX
A21F LD A,$FF No specific target - just moving
A221 LD ($B5D9),A
A224 CALL $8D27 Carry out the move
A227 POP IX Restore the river's own record
A229 RES 5,(IX+$07) Close it again
A22D POP HL Restore the original action
A22E LD ($B5D8),HL
A231 RET
