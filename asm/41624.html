A298: Handler for the crack: OPEN
The small insignificant crack can only be opened from room 15; there it opens like a door (9078).
A298 LD IX,($B5FC) IX = the actor's own record
A29C LD A,(IX+$10) Is the actor in room 15 (the only place the crack can be opened from)?
A29F CP $0F
A2A1 JP NZ,$9EBF No: fail with the usual message
A2A4 JP $9078 Yes: open it as usual
