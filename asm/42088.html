A468: CLIMB OUT OF
The default handler for CLIMB OUT OF. The actor must be inside the target, which must be open ('the barrel is closed'). The actor's holder is cleared: he is out.
A468 LD IY,($B5FC) IY = the actor's own record
A46C LD A,($B5D9) Is the actor actually inside the target?
A46F CP (IY+$01)
A472 JP NZ,$9EBF No: fail with the usual message
A475 CALL $9B25 IX = the target's own record
A478 CALL $A4C3 Is it open?
A47B JP Z,$A09C No: say so and stop
A47E CALL $9C99 Dry run? Then stop here: it would work
A481 LD (IY+$01),$FF The actor is held by nobody now: they are out
