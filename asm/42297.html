A539: Character routine: "where's the thief?"
If the character is in Bilbo's room but Bilbo cannot be seen (his visibility bit is clear), says 'where's the thief?'.
A539 LD A,($B5E7) A = the room the character started this turn in
A53C LD HL,$B5E6 Is Bilbo in that same room?
A53F CP (HL)
A540 RET NZ No: say nothing
A541 LD A,($C012) Yes - is Bilbo actually visible?
A544 BIT 7,A
A546 RET NZ He is: no need to ask
A547 CALL $9C99 Invisible: dry run? Then stop here: it always works
A54A LD HL,$AFBD "where's the thief?"
A54D JP $A113 Say it
