A577: Handler for the trap door: OPEN and CLOSE
The trap door can only be opened or closed from room 32; elsewhere 'you cannot reach the trap door'.
A577 LD IX,($B5FC) IX = the actor's own record
A57B LD A,(IX+$10) Is the actor in room 32 (the only place the trap door can be worked from)?
A57E CP $20
A580 LD HL,$B00D "you cannot reach the trap door."
A583 JP NZ,$72D4 No: say so and stop
A586 LD A,($B5D8) Yes - is this actually CLOSE (action 12)?
A589 CP $0C
A58B JP Z,$90A2 Yes: close it as usual
A58E JP $9078 Otherwise (OPEN): open it as usual
