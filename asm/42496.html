A600: Unused routine
Finds an exit of the actor's room by way of the variant search at 9E6E and, if there is one, removes its three bytes, presumably meaning to seal up a way through once something (perhaps the boat) is no longer there to use. But nothing in the game calls it: it is not wired into any object's handler list, any room-entry handler, or any timer, and no other routine calls or jumps to it. Like A0DE, it appears to be leftover or abandoned code.
