A614: Handler for the magic door
When the magic door is examined: if the actor can see it clearly (bit 7 of its flags), 'you see nothing special'. Otherwise timer 5 is started (by copying its reload value) and 'the magic door warns of elves approaching.'
A614 CALL $9C99 Dry run? Then stop here: it would work
A617 LD IX,($B5FC) IX = the actor's own record
A61B BIT 7,(IX+$07) Is the actor visible?
A61F LD HL,$AEE6 "X sees nothing special."
A622 JP NZ,$72D4 Visible: nothing more happens - just an ordinary EXAMINE
A625 LD A,($C996) Invisible (wearing the ring): start timer 5 (copy its reload value into its count)
A628 LD ($C997),A
A62B LD HL,$B19C "the magic door warns of elves approaching."
A62E JP $72D4
