A678: Handler for the window
Bilbo cannot reach the window unless something (or someone) is holding him up: if nothing holds him, 'you cannot reach the window.' Characters, and a Bilbo who is being carried, are passed on to the normal handlers for going through it (8E9D), breaking it (9257) or looking through it (8E4E). The HELP message in the goblins' dungeon hints at this: 'a window should be no obstacle to a thief with friends'.
A678 LD A,($B5DB) Is the actor Bilbo?
A67B CP $00
A67D JP NZ,$A68B No: characters can always reach it, below
A680 LD A,($C00C) Yes - is Bilbo currently being held up by something?
A683 CP $FF
A685 LD HL,$B00D "you cannot reach the window."
A688 JP Z,$72D4 Held by nobody: he cannot reach it - say so and stop
A68B LD A,($B5D8) A = the current action
A68E CP $1E Is it GO THROUGH?
A690 JP Z,$8E9D Yes: go through it as usual
A693 CP $0B Is it STRIKE (breaking it)?
A695 JP Z,$9257 Yes: break it as usual
A698 CP $18 Is it LOOK THROUGH?
A69A JP Z,$8E4E Yes: look through it as usual
A69D RET Anything else: do nothing further here
