A76A: Handler for the rope: PULL
If the boat is tied to the rope, pulling it brings it over: 'the boat glides across the river and lands on this side.' The boat moves between room 66 and room 67 (the east bank), is untied, and takes whatever is in it along (9B38). This is the way across the enchanted river - the HELP message at the river is 'boats can help. look carefully.'
