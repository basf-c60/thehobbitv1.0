A865: Character routine: dawn in the trolls' clearing
The trolls turn to stone at dawn. Both trolls (objects 71 and 72) are killed (96DD) and made invisible, and whatever they carried - including the large key - is left behind (9CA8). The trolls' clearing (room 5) gets a new description script, 'in a clearing with two stone trolls', and the game announces 'day dawns.'
The first two bytes of the room 5 picture (the border and paper colours, see 8BE9) are set to 5 and $28: cyan. At the start of a game (6C27) they are set to 0, so the clearing is drawn in black until dawn, and in daylight colours afterwards.
