A91B: Handler for the barrel: JUMP ONTO / CLIMB INTO
An actor can get into the barrel only from a room with an exit leading to where the barrel is, and only if that exit goes down (direction 10). Otherwise 'you cannot jump onto the barrel from here.' The actor then becomes held by the barrel (and so moves with it, 9B38).
A91B LD IY,($B5F8) IY = the barrel's own record
A91F LD A,(IY+$10) B = the room the barrel is in
A922 LD B,A
A923 CALL $9E76 Find an exit from the actor's own room that leads there
A926 CP $FF
A928 LD HL,$B1F3 "you cannot jump onto the barrel from here."
A92B JP Z,$72D4 No such exit: say so and stop
A92E LD A,(IX+$00) A = that exit's own direction
A931 CP $0A Is it DOWN?
A933 JP NZ,$B1F3 No: jump to the message text itself, rather than through the printer - see the bug note
A936 CALL $9C99 Dry run? Then stop here: it would work
A939 LD A,($B5D9) The actor becomes held by the barrel...
A93C LD IX,($B5FC)
A940 LD (IX+$01),A
A943 CALL $9B38 ...and moves wherever the barrel goes
A946 LD A,($B5DB) Is the actor Bilbo?
A949 CP $00
A94B RET NZ No: nothing more to do
A94C LD A,B Yes: describe the room the barrel is now in
A94D JP $958E
