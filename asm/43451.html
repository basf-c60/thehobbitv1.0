A9BB: Print a message if Bilbo is near the magic door
Prints the message at HL if Bilbo is in room 30 or room 28, the rooms on either side of the magic door.
A9BB LD A,($C01B) Is Bilbo in room 30...
A9BE CP $1E
A9C0 JP Z,$72D4 ...then print it
A9C3 CP $1C ...or room 28?
A9C5 JP Z,$72D4 ...then print it
A9C8 RET Otherwise say nothing
