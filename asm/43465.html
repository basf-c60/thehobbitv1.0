A9C9: Timer 5 expires: the magic door closes
The turn after the elf has swept past, the magic door closes again ('the magic door closes.', if Bilbo is near).
A9C9 LD HL,$C277 HL = the magic door's flags
A9CC RES 5,(HL) Close it
A9CE LD HL,$AF31 "the magic door closes."
A9D1 JP $A9BB Print it if Bilbo is nearby
