AA47: Action table
The 59 actions the game knows, 8 bytes each, numbered from 1 (70DF works out the address). Each entry is four word references, low byte first: the verb, a particle, a preposition, and a fourth word (GO for the ten movement actions). The parser's command is matched against these by 858F, so an action is really a verb plus the little words that go with it: TAKE, TAKE OUT OF, TAKE FROM and TAKE OFF are four different actions.
The top nibble of each word's high byte holds flags (collected by 70EA and decoded by 8569): whether a target and an instrument are needed, whether they can be rooms, whether the action can be done in the dark, and how it is reported.
Only the verbs appearing here do anything. Any other verb in the dictionary (WAIT, JUMP, SING...) just produces 'you wait. time passes...' (858F).
