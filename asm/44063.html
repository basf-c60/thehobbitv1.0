AC1F: Articles
7436 uses these to put an article in front of a noun. The first two bytes are unused. At AC21: THE, A, AN, SOME (chosen by flag bits in the noun's reference); at AC29: THE, THE, THE, SOME (used when a definite article is wanted).
AC1F DEFB $00,$00
AC21 DEFW $06A3 THE, A, AN, SOME
AC23 DEFW $0040
AC25 DEFW $0058
AC27 DEFW $0607
AC29 DEFW $06A3 THE, THE, THE, SOME
AC2B DEFW $06A3
AC2D DEFW $06A3
AC2F DEFW $0607
