AC31: Abbreviated words
The 32 most frequent words in messages, referenced by single bytes $60-$7F (see 748A). Each entry is a dictionary reference with $50 subtracted from its high byte. Using one byte instead of two for words such as 'the', 'you', 'is' and 'and' saves a lot of space across all the game's messages.
