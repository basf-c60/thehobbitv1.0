B60F: Verb endings
Six 4-byte endings added to verbs by 74B8 when the subject is not 'you': S, ES, IES, a backspace and IES (the backspace rubs out a final Y: 'carry' becomes 'carries'), D and ING. The rest of the 32 bytes are unused, and are followed by the orders buffer at B628.
