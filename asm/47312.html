B8D0: Room pointer table
Addresses of the records for rooms 0 to 79 (used by 9B0C). Room numbers are what appear in objects' location bytes and in the exits of other rooms. Some examples: 1 is Bilbo's comfortable tunnel-like hall, 2 is the forest road and 3 the forest (the 'pale bulbous eyes' rooms), 5 is the trolls' clearing, 7 is the trolls' cave, 22 is Beorn's house, 26 is the place of black spiders, 29 is the deep bog, 46 is the stretch of forest road west of the eyes, and 61 is where the golden ring starts.
