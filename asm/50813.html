C67D: Room-entry handlers
3-byte entries: a room number and the address of a routine to call when the player enters that room (see 8D19). Terminated by $FF.
  • Room 22 ($16, Beorn's house): C693 - switches the butler on
  • Room 26 ($1A, the place of black spiders): C6A1 - starts timer C981
  • Room 29 ($1D, the deep bog): C6A8 - starts timer C98F
  • Room 33 ($21, the forest river): C6D9 - death unless Bilbo is in the barrel
  • Room 2 ($02): C6CC - the pale bulbous eyes
  • Room 3 ($03): C6CC - the pale bulbous eyes
  • Room 32 ($20, the Elvenking's cellar): C6AF - switches the dragon and Bard on, and starts the side door timer

C685 DEFB $C6,$21,$D9,$C6,$02,$CC,$C6,$03
C68D DEFB $CC,$C6,$20,$AF,$C6
