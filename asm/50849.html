C6A1: Room-entry handler: the place of black spiders (room 26) starts timer 2
Starts timer 2 (count 5). Bilbo can stay for the turn he arrives and three more; if he is still in room 26 at the end of the next one, the spiders' web smothers him (AA04). The HELP message there is 'don't stay too long.'
C6A1 LD A,($C981) Timer 2's reload value (5)...
C6A4 LD ($C982),A ...becomes its count: the timer starts
