C6CC: Room-entry handler: the forest (rooms 2 and 3)
Called when Bilbo enters the forest road (room 2) or the forest (room 3). It records the room he has just entered (from $8D17, set by 8D19) in $B5E4, and starts the 'pale bulbous eyes' timer at C9AB by copying its reload value (4) into its count.
The rest of the mechanism is in AA13 and AA2E.
C6CC LD A,($8D17) Remember which room the timer started in
C6CF LD ($B5E4),A Store A in the room where the eyes timer started
C6D2 LD A,($C9AB) Start the eyes timer (count = 4)
C6D5 LD ($C9AC),A Store A in timer 8's count
C6D8 RET Done
