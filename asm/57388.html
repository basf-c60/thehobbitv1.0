E02C: Picture: room 13 (?)
The picture for room 13 (?). It is 29 bytes long: 10 lines, 2 moves, 1 fills and 0 painted areas. See 8985 for the format.
This picture has no end marker. Its last command is a move whose two coordinate bytes are the first two bytes (the colours) of the picture that follows, for room 31, so after drawing its own few details it carries straight on and draws the whole of that picture too, in its own colours. Two locations get pictures for little more than the price of one.
